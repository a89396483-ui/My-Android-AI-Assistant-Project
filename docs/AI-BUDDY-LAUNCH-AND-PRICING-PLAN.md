# AI Buddy launch and pricing plan

## Launch principle

Prepare the existing AI Buddy project as one simple, mobile-first product. Keep the current website and its existing features; add specialist roles in stages and test each stage before expanding.

## Initial free test version

- AI Buddy chat and basic multilingual help, subject to a working AI provider connection.
- Basic business and music marketing drafts for the owner.
- Basic customer-facing demo so a small number of people can test the workflow.
- Mini Software Builder for a small idea plan and starter code.
- Local chat history and clear-data controls already present in the app.

The owner's own AI Buddy and music marketing drafts are free to use in this initial plan. This means no customer service fee is charged to the owner for using the marketing workspace; any external AI provider usage or hosting charge must still be disclosed and approved before it is incurred.

## Customer trial and paid work

1. Let a small number of customers try a limited basic demo first.
2. Ask what worked, what failed and what result they actually need.
3. Before doing a larger customer job, show the exact scope, delivery, price and any third-party costs in plain language.
4. Start paid work only after the customer clearly agrees.
5. Keep the first paid offer small and easy to understand. Set the actual PKR price only after the first tests; no price is fixed in this document.
6. Do not collect payments inside the app until a payment method, refund process and security checks are deliberately added and tested.

## Paid and optional services

Keep expensive or provider-dependent features off by default: advanced AI models, paid image/video generation, music generation, automatic social posting and third-party account integrations. Show the provider and expected cost first, and require the owner's approval. Never silently spend money.

## Marketing rules

- The owner can use the marketing workspace for their own AI Buddy and music promotion without a customer service fee.
- Customer marketing work can become a paid service only after a free/basic test and a clear quote.
- Prepare drafts and schedules first. Do not publish, message leads or contact customers automatically without explicit owner approval.
- Never promise viral reach, guaranteed customers or guaranteed income.

## Release checklist

- Verify GitHub build, lint and tests on the current feature branch.
- Configure and test the server-side AI provider key without exposing it in browser code.
- Verify the live website and mobile layout.
- Test the free demo with a small group.
- Decide the first paid service and PKR price from actual feedback.
- Add payments and any automation only in a later approved stage.
