# AI Buddy Business Marketing

Status: specification.

## Goal
Acquire genuine users and business customers for AI Buddy, independently from music promotion.

## Workspace
Separate AI Buddy brand, Facebook Page, Instagram, YouTube presence, calendar, leads, analytics, and budget. Do not publish music material here by default.

## Workflow
Find a real customer problem -> create truthful demo -> draft platform-specific post -> verify claims and links -> owner approval -> publish through authorized API or provide manual export -> record receipt -> review inquiries and analytics.

## Content
Short product demos, feature explainers, simple Urdu/Hindi tutorials, permitted customer use cases, release notes, and transparent limitations/pricing.

## Leads
Use voluntarily supplied or legitimate public business contact information. Draft personalized replies; no bulk spam. Escalate complaints, refunds, legal questions, and pricing commitments.

## Truthfulness and metrics
Never claim unsupported features, guaranteed earnings, unlimited free access, or certifications. Track qualified inquiries, demo requests, activation, retention, support resolution, and feedback—not impressions alone.
