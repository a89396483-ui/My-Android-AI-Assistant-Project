# Master Marketing Architecture

Status: specification, not proof of live integrations.

## Separate workspaces
- AI Buddy Business Marketing: acquire genuine software users and customers.
- Music Promotion: promote Ashraf's own releases first, then authorized client artists.
Each has separate brand, accounts, calendars, audiences, assets, analytics, and budgets. Never cross-post by default.

## Ten participants
Eight internal role agents: Manager, Opportunity Finder, Customer Inbox, Software Builder, QA & Security, Marketing, Sales, Finance & Operations. Ashraf is owner/operator and ChatGPT is external reviewer. Total ten. Gemini is not an internal team member.

## Workflow
Brief and rights intake -> audience/channel plan -> content drafts -> QA -> owner approval -> durable scheduled queue -> official platform publishing -> confirmed receipt -> analytics -> learning report.

## Permissions
Default draft-only. Publishing requires authorized account connection and owner-approved policy. Paid spend always needs fresh approval specifying platform, account, amount, currency, objective, dates, and cap. No contracts, purchases, price changes, or bulk unsolicited messages without explicit approval. Provide a global pause that stops queued publishing and outbound automation.

## Social account separation
Keep AI Buddy business pages, Music Promotion service pages, and Ashraf's Artist accounts separate. Each client artist later gets a separate workspace. No content sharing between workspaces unless explicitly approved.

## Platform and FYP truth
Use official APIs and platform-approved flows. Publishing permissions, review, formats, quotas, and eligibility vary. If direct publishing is unavailable, prepare a manual upload package. Never claim success without a platform receipt. Organic For You placement cannot be guaranteed; optimize creative quality and learn from real analytics. Paid promotion is not guaranteed virality.

## Required system
Persistent database, durable queue and scheduler, OAuth integrations, encrypted token storage, asset rights records, approvals, publish receipts, analytics, audit log, pause control, workspace isolation, monitoring, and tests.

## Launch gates
No production publishing or ads until permissions, security, end-to-end tests, platform policy review, and owner acceptance are complete.
