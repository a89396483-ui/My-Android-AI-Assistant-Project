# Music Promotion & FYP Engine

Status: product specification; publishing and ad buying are not yet implemented.

## Pilot
Ashraf's own authorized music is the first campaign. Later support other artists only with written asset rights and account authorization.

## Campaign phases
- Pre-release: suggested 7-day preparation for release date, artist identity, artwork, rights, hooks, teasers, creator shortlist, and calendar.
- Release day: prepare launch clips, captions, official links, and approved posts.
- Post-release: suggested 30-day creative testing, creator collaborations, analytics, and weekly reports.

## Asset intake
Audio, lyrics, cover art, artist name, release date, distribution links, language, explicit-content status, territories, and usage rights. Confirm ownership/permission before use. Do not impersonate artists or fabricate endorsements.

## AI tasks
1. Analyze supplied audio and suggest 10-30 second hooks; owner confirms.
2. Create concepts for lyric videos, teasers, visualizers, short clips, and behind-the-scenes.
3. Draft platform-specific captions, subtitles, and hashtags.
4. Research public trends and relevant creators without scraping private data or copying protected work.
5. Draft collaboration messages; no bulk unsolicited DMs.
6. Prepare TikTok, Reels, Shorts, and Facebook calendars.
7. Track available views, retention, completion, shares, saves, comments, profile visits, link clicks, and sound use.
8. Compare creative variants and recommend next experiments.

## FYP strategy
Test strong openings, clear audio, readable subtitles, native formats, audience fit, and varied creative. Never use fake views, bots, engagement pods, or misleading metadata. Organic FYP is never guaranteed.

## Paid campaigns
AI may prepare proposal and creative. Execution requires fresh approval for platform, account, total budget, currency, schedule, targeting, objective, and maximum spend. Ads access must be verified separately from organic publishing.

## Client service
Separate client workspace, written scope, asset rights, account authorization, client budget, and exportable report. Never reuse one artist's private assets or data for another.
