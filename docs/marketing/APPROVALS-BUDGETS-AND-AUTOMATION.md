# Approvals, Budgets & Automation Policy

Status: required safety policy.

## Default
Draft-only. Agents may research legitimate public information, analyze owner-provided assets, prepare drafts, calendars, and reports.

## Publishing modes
1. Draft-only by default.
2. Owner-approved queue: each post needs approval before scheduling.
3. Policy-based auto-publish: only after owner configures named accounts, content types, platforms, limits, and time window. Sensitive posts always require approval.

## Fresh approval always required
Any paid ad, boost, purchase, subscription, budget change, contract, quote, refund, price commitment, legal statement, expanded OAuth scope, bulk outreach, production deployment, merge to main, or release.

## Approval record
Record actor, workspace, exact action/payload hash, target account, budget/currency, approver, timestamp, expiry, and outcome. Generic consent is insufficient.

## Global pause
Owner control pauses queued posts, outbound messages, and campaign execution. Preserve audit history. Resume requires owner action.

## Integrity
Respect rate limits, idempotency, duplicate checks, quiet hours, and account caps. No fake engagement, bots, spam, impersonation, or platform-control evasion.
