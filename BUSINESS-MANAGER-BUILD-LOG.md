## 14-day marketing target (target window: 2026-11-23 to 2026-11-30)

### Days 1–3: owner setup
- Confirm product/service name, target customers, public contact number, service area, pricing, and business hours.
- Prepare the business profile, FAQs, offer, and owner handoff rule.
- Confirm which social channels are available and authorized.

### Days 4–6: marketing materials
- Create approved brand visuals, short posts, offer copy, and a simple landing-page description.
- Prepare customer reply templates and a capability list that describes only working services.
- Owner reviews and approves all public claims and contact details.

### Days 7–10: channel setup and testing
- Connect only selected channels through their supported official integrations.
- Test inbound enquiries and owner handoff with test contacts.
- Keep outbound messages and paid promotions approval-gated.

### Days 11–14: controlled launch
- Publish the first owner-approved posts on connected channels.
- Review incoming enquiries daily and track leads in the dashboard.
- Do not buy ads or send bulk messages without a separately approved budget and channel permission.

## Launch dependency

A real marketing launch by the target window depends on the owner supplying the reference design, business identity, offer, contact details, target audience, and the channels to use, plus successful authorized channel connections. The date is a target, not a guarantee.
